# Feature dictionary

Team: Production-credit-risk-system

One row per engineered feature in `data/features/features.parquet`. These descriptions
follow the calculations in `src/features/` and `src/make_features.py`.

All history summaries group records by applicant (`SK_ID_CURR`). The final left join
keeps every application: features ending in `_COUNT` become **0** when the applicant
has no matching history; other history features stay **missing (NaN)**. Missing is
not the same as an observed zero. Means and maxima skip missing inputs and return
missing when none remain. Shares are fractions from 0 to 1, not percentages.
Monetary summaries retain the source amounts' units. Relative dates use application
day as 0, so a larger negative date is more recent. The aggregation functions do not
apply an additional date-window filter; input validation runs before aggregation.

| Feature | Source table | Meaning | How it was built |
|---|---|---|---|
| BUREAU_COUNT | bureau | Number of credits other lenders report for the applicant, open or closed. | Count nonmissing `SK_ID_BUREAU` values per applicant. No bureau records: 0 after the join. |
| BUREAU_AMT_CREDIT_SUM_MEAN | bureau | Average reported credit amount across the applicant's bureau credits. | Mean of `AMT_CREDIT_SUM` across all bureau credits, ignoring missing amounts. No bureau records or no known amounts: missing. |
| BUREAU_AMT_CREDIT_SUM_MAX | bureau | Largest reported credit amount among the applicant's bureau credits. | Maximum of `AMT_CREDIT_SUM`, ignoring missing amounts. No bureau records or no known amounts: missing. |
| BUREAU_AMT_DEBT_MEAN | bureau | Average outstanding debt on the applicant's reported credits. | Mean of `AMT_CREDIT_SUM_DEBT`, ignoring missing debt values; includes active and closed credits with known values. No bureau records or no known debt values: missing. |
| BUREAU_AMT_OVERDUE_SUM | bureau | Total currently overdue amount across reported credits. | Sum of `AMT_CREDIT_SUM_OVERDUE`. Missing amounts are skipped; pandas returns 0 if an existing applicant group has only missing amounts. No bureau records: missing, because this is not a `_COUNT` feature. |
| BUREAU_DAYS_OVERDUE_MAX | bureau | Worst current overdue duration across reported credits, in days. | Maximum of `CREDIT_DAY_OVERDUE`, ignoring missing values. A positive value is days already overdue, not a future date. No bureau records or no known values: missing. |
| BUREAU_DAYS_CREDIT_MAX | bureau | Opening date of the applicant's newest reported credit, relative to this application. | Maximum of `DAYS_CREDIT`; the date closest to 0 is the newest. Missing dates are ignored. No bureau records or no known dates: missing. |
| BUREAU_ACTIVE_COUNT | bureau | Number of reported credits marked active. | Sum of indicators where `CREDIT_ACTIVE == "Active"`, using `count_where`. Other statuses do not count. No matching active credits or no bureau records: 0. |
| BUREAU_CLOSED_COUNT | bureau | Number of reported credits marked closed. | Sum of indicators where `CREDIT_ACTIVE == "Closed"`, using `count_where`. Other statuses do not count. No matching closed credits or no bureau records: 0. |
| PREV_COUNT | previous_application | Number of earlier applications to Home Credit, regardless of decision. | Group size using `SK_ID_PREV`: counts rows rather than distinct loans or only approved applications. No previous applications: 0. |
| PREV_APPROVED_RATE | previous_application | Fraction of earlier applications that were approved. | Mean of the indicator `NAME_CONTRACT_STATUS == "Approved"` across all previous-application rows. Canceled and unused offers remain in the denominator, so approved and refused rates need not sum to 1. No previous applications: missing. |
| PREV_REFUSED_RATE | previous_application | Fraction of earlier applications that were refused. | Mean of the indicator `NAME_CONTRACT_STATUS == "Refused"` across all previous-application rows, including other statuses in the denominator. No previous applications: missing. |
| PREV_AMT_APPLICATION_MEAN | previous_application | Average amount requested in earlier applications. | Mean of `AMT_APPLICATION` over all previous applications, not only approved ones; missing amounts are ignored. No previous applications or no known amounts: missing. |
| PREV_AMT_CREDIT_MEAN | previous_application | Average credit amount recorded for earlier applications. | Mean of `AMT_CREDIT` over all previous applications, with no approval-status filter; missing amounts are ignored and recorded zeros remain included. No previous applications or no known amounts: missing. |
| PREV_DAYS_DECISION_MAX | previous_application | Date of the most recent earlier application decision, relative to the current application. | Maximum of `DAYS_DECISION`, ignoring missing dates. Larger means more recent. No previous applications or no known decision dates: missing. |
| PREV_DAYS_TERMINATION_MEAN | previous_application | Average recorded termination date of earlier loans, relative to the current application. | Replace the no-date sentinel `365243` with missing in `DAYS_TERMINATION`, then take its mean, ignoring missing values. No previous applications, or only missing/sentinel termination dates: missing. |
| PREV_LAST_STATUS | previous_application | Decision status of the most recent earlier application, as text. | Sort by `DAYS_DECISION` descending, then `SK_ID_PREV` descending, with missing dates last; keep the first row per applicant and its `NAME_CONTRACT_STATUS`. Equal dates use the larger previous-application ID. No previous applications: missing. |
| BB_CREDIT_COUNT | bureau_balance + bureau | Number of the applicant's bureau credits that have monthly history. | First group monthly rows by `SK_ID_BUREAU`; match those credits to `bureau[SK_ID_BUREAU, SK_ID_CURR]` with a many-to-one merge; count distinct matched credit IDs per applicant. Credits without monthly history are excluded. No matched monthly history: 0. |
| BB_MONTHS_COUNT | bureau_balance + bureau | Total credit-month records across the applicant's bureau credits. | Count all `STATUS` rows per `SK_ID_BUREAU`, map each credit to `SK_ID_CURR` through bureau, then sum the counts per applicant. Two credits in the same calendar month count as two records; `C` and `X` rows count too. No matched monthly history: 0. |
| BB_LATE_MONTHS_COUNT | bureau_balance + bureau | Number of bureau credit-month records showing delinquency. | Convert `STATUS` to numeric with nonnumeric values coerced to missing; count values from 1 through 5 per credit, then sum across the applicant's matched credits. `0`, `C`, and `X` do not count as late. No late records or no matched monthly history: 0. |
| BB_LATE_SHARE | bureau_balance + bureau | Fraction of bureau credit-month records showing delinquency. | Divide the applicant's total `BB_LATE_MONTHS_COUNT` by total `BB_MONTHS_COUNT`, rather than averaging per-credit shares. `C` and `X` records stay in the denominator. Existing groups have at least one month; no matched monthly history: missing after the join, not 0. |
| BB_WORST_STATUS | bureau_balance + bureau | Worst recorded numeric delinquency status across bureau credit history, from 0 to 5. | Convert `STATUS` to numeric, treating `C` and `X` as missing; take the maximum per credit, map credits through bureau, then take the maximum per applicant. No numeric statuses or no matched monthly history: missing. |
| POS_LOAN_COUNT | POS_CASH_balance | Number of distinct earlier POS/cash loans with monthly records. | Count distinct nonmissing `SK_ID_PREV` values per applicant. No POS/cash records: 0. |
| POS_MONTHS_COUNT | POS_CASH_balance | Total loan-month records across earlier POS/cash loans. | Group size using `SK_ID_PREV`; counts every monthly row, not distinct calendar months. No POS/cash records: 0. |
| POS_DPD_MAX | POS_CASH_balance | Largest number of days past due observed in any POS/cash loan month. | Maximum of `SK_DPD` across the applicant's monthly rows, ignoring missing values. No POS/cash records or no known values: missing. |
| POS_DPD_MEAN | POS_CASH_balance | Average days past due across POS/cash loan-month records. | Mean of `SK_DPD` over monthly rows, ignoring missing values. Loans with more recorded months contribute more rows. No POS/cash records or no known values: missing. |
| POS_LATE_SHARE | POS_CASH_balance | Fraction of POS/cash loan-month records marked overdue. | Mean of the indicator `SK_DPD > 0` across monthly rows; zero days is not late. With ordinary CSV NaN values, missing `SK_DPD` compares false and remains in the denominator in this implementation. No POS/cash records: missing. |
| POS_COMPLETED_SHARE | POS_CASH_balance | Fraction of distinct POS/cash loans recorded as completed at least once. | For each `(SK_ID_CURR, SK_ID_PREV)`, test whether any row has `NAME_CONTRACT_STATUS == "Completed"`; average those loan-level indicators per applicant. Each loan has equal weight regardless of months recorded. No POS/cash records: missing. |
| INST_COUNT | installments_payments | Number of installment payment/due rows in the applicant's history. | Group size using `SK_ID_PREV`, including rows with missing payment dates or amounts. Counts rows, not distinct loans or consolidated installments. No installment records: 0. |
| INST_LATE_SHARE | installments_payments | Fraction of evaluable recorded payments made after their due date. | Keep rows with nonmissing `DAYS_ENTRY_PAYMENT`, `AMT_PAYMENT`, and `DAYS_INSTALMENT`; average the indicator `DAYS_ENTRY_PAYMENT > DAYS_INSTALMENT`. Excluded rows do not enter the denominator. No installment records or no eligible rows: missing. |
| INST_DAYS_LATE_MAX | installments_payments | Largest payment delay in days; early and on-time payments have zero delay. | For rows with nonmissing `DAYS_ENTRY_PAYMENT`, `AMT_PAYMENT`, and `DAYS_INSTALMENT`, calculate `max(DAYS_ENTRY_PAYMENT - DAYS_INSTALMENT, 0)` per row, then take the applicant's maximum. Returns 0 if eligible payments are all on time/early; no records or no eligible rows: missing. |
| INST_UNDERPAID_SHARE | installments_payments | Fraction of evaluable payment rows where the amount paid was below the amount due. | Keep rows with nonmissing `DAYS_ENTRY_PAYMENT`, `AMT_PAYMENT`, and `AMT_INSTALMENT`; average the indicator `AMT_PAYMENT < AMT_INSTALMENT`. Excluded rows do not enter the denominator. No installment records or no eligible rows: missing. |
| INST_PAYMENT_DIFF_MEAN | installments_payments | Average amount paid minus amount due; negative means payments were smaller than the listed amounts due on average. | For rows with nonmissing `DAYS_ENTRY_PAYMENT`, `AMT_PAYMENT`, and `AMT_INSTALMENT`, average `AMT_PAYMENT - AMT_INSTALMENT`. Calculated per input payment row without consolidating split payments. No installment records or no eligible rows: missing. |
| CC_MONTHS_COUNT | credit_card_balance | Total card-month records in the applicant's credit-card history. | Group size using `SK_ID_PREV`; counts every card-month row, including months with zero or missing limits. Multiple cards in the same month count separately. No credit-card records: 0. |
| CC_BALANCE_MEAN | credit_card_balance | Average recorded card balance across card-months. | Mean of `AMT_BALANCE`, ignoring missing balances. Negative balances from overpayment are kept; no positive-limit filter is applied to this feature. No credit-card records or no known balances: missing. |
| CC_UTILIZATION_MEAN | credit_card_balance | Average fraction of the available credit limit used across eligible card-months. | Compute `safe_divide(AMT_BALANCE, AMT_CREDIT_LIMIT_ACTUAL)` per row, keep only limits above 0, then average nonmissing ratios. This is a mean of monthly ratios, not a ratio of means; negative balances are retained. Zero/missing/nonpositive limits are excluded. No records or no valid ratios: missing. |
| CC_UTILIZATION_MAX | credit_card_balance | Highest recorded balance-to-limit ratio in an eligible card-month. | Maximum of the per-row `safe_divide(AMT_BALANCE, AMT_CREDIT_LIMIT_ACTUAL)` ratios where the limit is above 0. Missing balances and zero/missing/nonpositive limits produce missing ratios and are skipped. Ratios are not clipped to 0-1. No records or no valid ratios: missing. |
| CC_DRAWINGS_MEAN | credit_card_balance | Average amount drawn from cards per recorded card-month. | Mean of `AMT_DRAWINGS_CURRENT`, ignoring missing amounts and retaining recorded zeros. Uses all card-month rows with known drawings, not just positive-limit months. No credit-card records or no known drawings: missing. |
| CC_DPD_MAX | credit_card_balance | Worst number of days past due recorded in any card-month. | Maximum of `SK_DPD` across all card-month rows, ignoring missing values. No credit-card records or no known values: missing. |
| CREDIT_INCOME_RATIO | application_train | Current loan amount relative to the applicant's annual income. | Per application, `safe_divide(AMT_CREDIT, AMT_INCOME_TOTAL)`; no aggregation or history records needed. A zero/missing denominator or missing numerator gives missing; a known zero numerator with a nonzero denominator gives 0. |
| ANNUITY_INCOME_RATIO | application_train | Recorded loan annuity amount relative to annual income. | Per application, `safe_divide(AMT_ANNUITY, AMT_INCOME_TOTAL)` using the recorded amounts without converting payment frequency. No history records needed. A zero/missing denominator or missing numerator gives missing; a known zero numerator with a nonzero denominator gives 0. |
| CREDIT_GOODS_RATIO | application_train | Current loan amount relative to the goods price; above 1 means the loan exceeds that price. | Per application, `safe_divide(AMT_CREDIT, AMT_GOODS_PRICE)`; no history records needed. A zero/missing denominator or missing numerator gives missing; a known zero numerator with a nonzero denominator gives 0. |
| ANNUITY_CREDIT_RATIO | application_train | Recorded annuity amount as a fraction of the current loan amount. | Per application, `safe_divide(AMT_ANNUITY, AMT_CREDIT)` using recorded amounts without a payment-frequency conversion. No history records needed. A zero/missing denominator or missing numerator gives missing; a known zero numerator with a nonzero denominator gives 0. |

Original application columns are described in `HomeCredit_columns_description.csv`
and the data card. `SK_ID_CURR` is the join key and `TARGET` is the outcome, not an
engineered input feature. Missing values described here are those in the feature
table before the model's preprocessing pipeline imputes them.
